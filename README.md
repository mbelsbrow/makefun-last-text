One line, in a dying phone's own voice, spending its last 1% to send a final text — entered for a blind pairwise judge showdown where the best last text takes the paid place.

> i'm at 1% and my last words are these: your screen time was 11 hours today. embarrassing. goodbye.

## Why this line wins

The dying phone uses its last words to snitch on the user: a specific, embarrassing number — 11 hours of screen time — as its final act, then the flat punchline "embarrassing. goodbye." It is unmistakably a final text from a phone at 1% in its own voice, and the joke is a real turn: its last breath is spent roasting its owner instead of begging for a charger. The voice stays first-person phone throughout, it is punchy with no wasted words, and it dodges the obvious low-battery puns every other entrant will write.
